How the battle works

Letters: the admin gives both players the same letters each round.

Words: use only those letters; reuse is allowed, but each word can be played once per round.

Points: longer words earn more points and fire your tank.

Defence: armor absorbs damage first, then health. Zero health ends the match.

Specials: block, drain, steal, switch or clear skills.

Winning: knock out the opponent or lead on points when the rounds end.
