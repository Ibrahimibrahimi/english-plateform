How to play

  1. Each round gives both players the same letters chosen by the admin.
  2. Type an English word using only those letters, without repeating a letter.
  3. Longer words earn more points and fire your tank at the opponent.
  4. Armor absorbs damage first, then health. Zero health ends the match.
  5. After all rounds, the player with more points wins; ties trigger sudden death.
  6. Ctrl+0 special move, Ctrl+1 health, Ctrl+2 armor.
